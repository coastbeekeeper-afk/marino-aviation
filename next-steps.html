Before lesson one

Next steps for Part 61 training at SNA.

Books on the table. Medical in hand. TSA sorted. Then we fly. This is the short list Sam expects serious students to start — not a scavenger hunt.

01 — Library

The books (and the EFB)

Use current editions. Paper or digital is fine; what matters is that you actually read them before you waste dual on questions the PHAK already answered.

PHAK Pilot’s Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge

The knowledge backbone. Read it like you mean to pass the written — because you do. FAA PHAK

AFH Airplane Flying Handbook

How the airplane is flown, not just how it is explained. Pair it with every new maneuver. FAA Airplane Flying Handbook

FAR / AIM Federal Aviation Regulations / Aeronautical Information Manual

The rules and the system you are flying in. Keep a current copy in the bag. AIM · 14 CFR

ACS Airman Certification Standards

This is the test. Training is aimed at the ACS for the certificate you want — not at folklore. FAA ACS

EFB / charts Electronic Flight Bag and current charts

Bring a current EFB (ForeFlight, Garmin Pilot, or equivalent) with sectional / TAC coverage for Southern California, plus a backup. Paper charts are still useful when the glass goes dark.

02 — Medical

See an AME before you stack dual.

Do not wait until you are twenty hours in to discover a medical issue. For most Part 61 airplane training, plan on an FAA medical certificate issued by an Aviation Medical Examiner.

Find an examiner near Orange County with the FAA locator, book the exam, and bring the result to your first briefing.

FAA Aviation Medical Examiner locator

03 — TSA

U.S. citizens and AFSP are different paths.

U.S. citizens and nationals are not processed through the Alien Flight Student Program. You will still verify identity with your instructor (typically a U.S. passport or birth certificate plus government photo ID) before flight training begins.

If you are not a U.S. citizen, you must be approved through the TSA Alien Flight Student Program (AFSP) before flight training can start. Do not show up for dual hoping it can be skipped.
